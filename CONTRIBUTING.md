# Contributing to Krusader

Contributions are welcome! There are [multiple ways to contribute](https://docs.kde.org/trunk_kf6/en/krusader/krusader/help_krusader.html).

As a contributor, you are responsible for understanding and describing your changes.

## AI policy

There is the problem of AI ("Artificial Intelligence") programs that copy source code when there are no legal rights to do so.

Any submission that includes code that is produced by a program (e.g. an AI program) is not allowed, unless it meets any one of the following conditions:

- It is [too minimal to qualify for copyright protection](https://www.gnu.org/prep/maintain/html_node/Legally-Significant.html);
- It is clearly based on code that is published under a compatible license.

There are legal issues that [Codeberg.org mentions](https://codeberg.org/ethical-foss/ai-free-foss-alternatives/src/branch/main/why_not_llms.md): _"LLMs are often trained on, and thus prone to regurgitate (either completely, or in-part), chunks of code that are licensed under terms which have specific legal requirements that a contributor using LLMs may not understand or even be aware of when making a contribution"_.

Please, note that, in the case of Krusader, there are no legal problems when using AI tools for single-line autocompletion of source code, to look for errors in source code, to try to know the advantages of alternative approaches, to correct spelling, to translate comments into English and to perform similar tasks (always with a human reviewing the output generated by these tools, because, as these programs warn us, "this information may contain errors").
